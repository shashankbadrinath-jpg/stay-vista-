# 5-minute pitch script (about 4:50 read aloud)

**1. Who you are (45s)**
"I'm Shashank, an Associate Product Manager at Perfios in Bengaluru. I work on regulated fintech: Account Aggregator products across FIU, FIP and Consent Manager, with ReBIT specs and DPDP compliance, for enterprise BFSI clients. I have an MBA from Christ University, and I've worked in a founder's office, at Unacademy and at Brillio. I turn messy operational workflows into products people actually use. Today I'm pitching StayDesk."

**2. Best work (30s)**  (FILL IN YOUR OWN NUMBER)
"At Perfios I [shipped X], which [moved metric Y by Z / cut time from A to B]." One line of context, one number, one proof point on screen.

**3. Why StayDesk wins (1 min)**
"Boutique villa and homestay operators sell on WhatsApp. A guest asks, someone checks a spreadsheet, quotes by hand, and double bookings happen. Claude can read that message and write the perfect quote, but it forgets when the chat ends. It can't hold dates for 24 hours, release them on time, give a guest a link, or be the shared calendar a team trusts. StayDesk is that memory and calendar. The host's own Claude plan does the thinking, so I pay nothing for AI."

**4. Demo (2 min)**
1. Dashboard on the Calendar tab on one side, Claude on the other.
2. In Claude: "Family of 6, 26 to 29 December, Coorg. Hold the best option for Priya, 98450 12345, for 48 hours and write her a quote."
3. Show the tool calls, then the amber bar appearing on the calendar. Open the booking: Claude's message is there, editable.
4. Edit a line of the message and the total, Save. Copy guest link and open it: this is what Priya sees.
5. Back in Claude: "Confirm it." The bar turns teal. Then "Sales summary for this month."

**5. The money (45s)**
"Cost per customer is close to zero: one small server shared by everyone, roughly ₹800 a month, plus a few rupees of storage per host. Zero AI spend. I charge ₹1,499 a month per host, about 99% gross margin. First 100 customers: my own network of stay operators, then host WhatsApp groups in Coorg, Gokarna and Lonavala with a free 30-day desk, plus partnerships with villa photographers and channel managers who already talk to hosts. Year 1: 300 paying hosts, about ₹54 lakh ARR. Year 3: 3,000 hosts, with payment links, OTP login and Airbnb sync added, about ₹5 crore ARR."

Before recording: the rupee figures are my suggestions, so change anything you can't defend. Keep your own number for "best work".
